About me

Blending 10+ years of visual design with UX expertise to create user-centered, business-driven solutions. I lead end-to-end design processes, innovate with AI, and thrive in cross-functional teams to build scalable, impactful experiences.

2. Leadership & Collaboration

I lead workshops with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to gather insights, align visions, and co-create solutions, fostering strong collaboration across disciplines.

3. Data-Driven Decision Making

I leverage research insights and data analysis to identify opportunities, validate hypotheses, and drive design decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.

4. User-Centered Design

I prioritize user needs by creating intuitive, accessible, and consistent experiences, reusing familiar patterns and components to enhance usability and engagement.

5. Technical Fluency & Delivery Focus

I collaborate closely with development teams and design system teams to ensure that design solutions are technically feasible, scalable, and efficiently delivered.

6. Research & Benchmarking Expertise

I conduct audits and competitive benchmarking to uncover best practices and innovation opportunities, continually pushing for product and experience improvement.
